Travel guide

How to compare flights

When comparing flights, look beyond the headline price. Check departure and arrival times, total journey duration, number of stops, baggage conditions and the fare details.

Compare what matters

A cheaper fare is not always the most convenient option. Consider the complete journey and the conditions attached to the fare before continuing.

Before you book

Review the passenger names, dates, route and total price carefully. Keep your confirmation information available after booking.
