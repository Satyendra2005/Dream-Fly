Frequently asked questions

How do I search for a flight?

Enter your origin, destination, dates, passengers and cabin class, then select Search Flights.

Can I search one-way flights?

Yes. Select One Way in the flight search panel.

What does nonstop mean?

A nonstop flight travels from the departure airport to the destination without a scheduled stop.

What if I have a payment problem?

Use the Support Center and select Payment problem for guidance. If it does not solve the issue, contact support.

How do I request help?

Use the support chat in the bottom-right corner or visit the Support Center.
